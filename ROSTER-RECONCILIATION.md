# Roster reconciliation from NBA 2K

NBA 2K is the source for completed signings and waivers. LEAGUEbuddy records verified results; it does not run a second bidding market.

In the website admin workspace, open **Update rosters from 2K**. Upload Signing and Waive Transaction Report photos (including DATE, TYPE and NEWS), review player/team/date matches, resolve same-day conflicts, then separately confirm Save roster updates. HEIC and cropped reports are supported. Unknown players require explicit creation with name and primary position. This imports transaction reports, not whole-team roster screenshots.

Released players return to the free agent pool. Permanent player IDs and statistics are preserved. Repeated photos and older events cannot replay or rewind verified updates. Contract years and total dollars are optional; annual salaries and options are never inferred from a total. Every save has a backup, audit entry and retained original evidence.

Use **Offseason roster review** to review current rosters and confirm the trades, free agency or cutdown step. There are no exclusive bidding periods, offer priorities, custom offer scores, winner timers, signing quotas, conditional-release reservations, offer approvals, or coach waiver controls. Regular-season preparation and cutdowns allow 14–15 players. Contract salary verification is optional and does not block rollover. Trading retains its own proof and approval process.

`/freeagents` remains private read-only browsing. The old free agency channel and offer proof channel are removed only by the explicit commissioner retirement action. Shared Staff/trade channels are preserved. Before removing a dedicated channel, the cleanup backs up storage and saves its message transcript. Historical offers, completed transactions and proof originals remain on disk; pending work is cancelled without moving any player. Saved old buttons only explain the replacement workflow.

The authenticated retirement endpoint requires a prepare/confirm token, commissioner identity, a matching guild binding and unchanged source data. It never runs on a startup timer. New channel setup cannot recreate the retired channel.
